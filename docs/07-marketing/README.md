# Marketing and monetization

This folder describes how **Before I Buy** could become a sustainable business after the core social loop has been validated.

The strategy borrows proven patterns from social and creator platforms—subscriptions, community memberships, identity cosmetics, appreciation, creator tools, and carefully governed brand partnerships—but adapts them to a product whose credibility depends on neutral purchase reflection.

## Recommendation

Do not monetize during the initial validation or closed beta. First prove that people create authentic dilemmas, friends participate, owners close the loop, and Reveals produce return behavior.

When those signals are credible, sequence monetization as follows:

1. **Future You Plus:** a consumer subscription for richer private memory, insights, expression, and circle features.
2. **Circle Pass:** one person funds premium benefits for a private group.
3. **Cosmetic expression and gifting:** optional one-time purchases that never affect prediction scores or reach.
4. **Creator Circles:** paid communities and creator tools only after a safe public/creator surface is validated.
5. **Clearly disclosed commercial programs:** brand-funded challenges or post-decision affiliate links only with independent ranking, explicit labels, and strict trust guardrails.

Advertising inside private dilemmas and selling behavioral purchase data are not recommended.

## Core rule

The product may monetize **memory, expression, community, convenience, and creation tools**. It must not monetize pressure to buy, shame about spending, hidden persuasion, or the sale of private dilemmas.

## Relationship to the MVP

This is a post-validation strategy, not an expansion of the current MVP. The free product must continue to support the complete loop:

**Create → invite → vote → decide → reflect → Reveal**

No user should have to pay to see the outcome of a dilemma in which they participated.
